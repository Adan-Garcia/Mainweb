FRC Robotics

Intake Design 2023-2025

Visit Website ← Back to Projects

As part of FIRST Robotics Competition Team 340 (Greater Rochester Robotics), this work covered the design and assembly of custom robot mechanisms and intake systems, modeled in Onshape and built under the fast turnaround of a competitive high school robotics season.

The scope spanned both hardware and software: designing complex mechanical systems for game-piece intake, then writing and optimizing the autonomous routines and tele-operated controls that ran on them. Getting a robot from CAD to competition-ready meant collaborating closely with the rest of the team in a fast-paced environment, season after season from 2023 to 2025.

Highlights

  • Designed and assembled custom intake mechanisms using Onshape
  • Wrote and optimized autonomous routines and tele-operated controls
  • Collaborated within a team to deliver competition-ready robots each season
  • Mentored newer members on safety and design principles
