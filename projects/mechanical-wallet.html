Mechanical Wallet

3D-Printed Card Ejector & Money Clip

← Back to Projects

This is an ergonomic, slim-profile mechanical wallet modeled in Fusion 360 and Onshape, with every part designed around the constraints of 3D printing.

A lever at the base pushes the stored cards up into a staggered fan, so any card can be reached with one hand. A detachable money clip snaps onto the back of the shell with hooked ends and uses the flex of the printed plastic itself as its spring, so it needs no extra hardware.

Highlights

  • Modeled in Fusion 360 and Onshape, designed for 3D printing from the start
  • Lever-driven ejector fans the cards out for one-handed access
  • Snap-on money clip that uses the printed plastic as a flexure spring
