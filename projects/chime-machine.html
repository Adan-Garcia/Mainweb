Chime Machine

Automated Electromechanical Instrument

← Back to Projects

Chime Machine is an automated electromechanical instrument designed and fabricated by a four-person team. The mechanical assemblies were modeled in Onshape, and the machine strikes physical chimes on its own to play a 6-note, 59-second arrangement of "Aria Math".

I led the C++ Arduino programming and system tuning, coordinating 2 motors and 4 solenoids so every strike lands on time. Through iterative testing, we identified issues affecting the acoustics and implemented solutions to improve the sound quality and timing of the instrument.

Highlights

  • Designed mechanical assemblies in Onshape as part of a four-person team
  • Led C++ Arduino programming to synchronize 2 motors and 4 solenoids
  • Tuned timing and acoustics through iterative testing
