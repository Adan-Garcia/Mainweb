Chime Machine

Designed and fabricated an automated electromechanical instrument in a four-person team. I led the C++ Arduino programming and system tuning, coordinating 2 motors and 4 solenoids to play a 6-note, 59-second arrangement of "Aria Math". Through iterative testing, we identified issues affecting the aocustics and implemented solutions to improve the sound quality and timing of the instrument.
